# Initial Wedge

Ovara should enter the market through a narrow, painful, high-frequency use
case: autonomous coding and DevOps execution.

## Why This Wedge

- agents already execute shell commands constantly
- repository mutation is high-value and easy to understand
- deployment authority is consequential and approval-sensitive
- buyers already feel the pain without needing category education

## V1 Action Classes

- `shell.exec`
- `git.push`
- `github.pull_request.merge`
- `cicd.deploy.trigger`
- `approval.request`

## What We Deliberately Ignore At First

- general browser automation
- finance and payments
- healthcare workflows
- broad cross-SaaS orchestration
- advanced identity federation

Winning the initial wedge matters more than appearing comprehensive.
